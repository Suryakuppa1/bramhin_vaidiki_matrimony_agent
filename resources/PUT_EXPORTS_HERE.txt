Put your chat exports in this folder (resources/).

Supported for import:
  - .txt / .text  (Telegram copy-paste, WhatsApp "Export chat" without media)
  - .json         (Telegram Desktop machine-readable export)

Not imported yet (skipped with a note):
  - images (.jpg/.png/…), PDF, Word, audio/video

Suggested layout:
  resources/telegram_group_a.txt
  resources/whatsapp_group_b.txt
  resources/old_export.json

Each biodata block should use labeled lines, for example:

  CONSENT:YES
  Name: Example Name
  DOB: 14-07-1999
  Nakshatram: Rohini
  Rashi: Vrishabha
  Place of Birth: Hyderabad
  Education: B.Tech

Telugu labels (పేరు, నక్షత్రం, …) also work.

Then run:
  python import_resources.py --i-have-permission

Only import people you are allowed to process. Your exports are gitignored.
